## 👋 About Me

I'm a developer who builds across the full stack — from responsive frontends and scalable backends to machine learning pipelines and on-chain smart contracts. I enjoy tackling hard problems at the intersection of AI, cloud infrastructure, and decentralized systems.

- 🔭 I work with **TypeScript**, **Python**, and **Solidity** day-to-day
- 🤖 Passionate about **AI/ML** — from classical models to deep learning and LLM integrations
- ☁️ I deploy on **AWS** and leverage serverless + container-based architectures
- ⛓️ Exploring **Web3** — smart contracts, on-chain verification, and DeFi patterns
- 🚀 I love shipping things fast with **React**, **Next.js**, **FastAPI**, and **Supabase**
